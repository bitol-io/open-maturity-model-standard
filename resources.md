## Sibling standards

* [ODCS — Open Data Contract Standard](https://github.com/bitol-io/open-data-contract-standard)
* [ODPS — Open Data Product Standard](https://github.com/bitol-io/open-data-product-standard)
* [OORS — Open Observability Results Standard](https://github.com/bitol-io/open-observability-results-standard)

OMMS reuses the ODCS data quality vocabulary (`metric`, `arguments`, operators, `unit`) for its
criteria, so the [ODCS Data Quality documentation](https://bitol-io.github.io/open-data-contract-standard/latest/data-quality/)
is the reference for what a metric and its arguments mean.
