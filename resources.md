## Sibling standards

* [ODCS — Open Data Contract Standard](https://github.com/bitol-io/open-data-contract-standard)
* [ODPS — Open Data Product Standard](https://github.com/bitol-io/open-data-product-standard)
* [OORS — Open Observability Results Standard](https://github.com/bitol-io/open-observability-results-standard)

OMMS criteria started from the ODCS data quality vocabulary. Since v0.2.1 they use two metrics of
their own, `valueOf` and `count`, documented in [Criteria](docs/criteria.md); the
[ODCS Data Quality documentation](https://bitol-io.github.io/open-data-contract-standard/latest/data-quality/)
remains the reference for ODCS rules themselves.
